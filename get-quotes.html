Questions about getting quotes

Why three quotes?

Three quotes give you a reliable sense of the local price per watt without drowning you in calls. Price spreads between installers for the same system are common, so comparing pays.

Does Be.Solar install systems?

No. We are an independent publisher. Partner installers may pay a referral fee when we send them a request. That fee never changes the price you're quoted and never affects our calculators or guides.

I rent. Can I still use solar?

Often yes, through community solar subscriptions, balcony or plug-in kits where allowed, or by sharing a proposal with your landlord. Submit the form and choose "I rent" and we'll point you to options in your area.
