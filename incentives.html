Solar incentives by country

Where things stand in October 2026, with links to the official sources. Rules change often; always confirm before you sign.

CountryMain homeowner incentive nowStatus and deadlinesOur guide
United StatesNo federal credit for homeowner-owned systems. Leases and PPAs may carry a commercial credit claimed by the provider. State, utility and local programs vary.Residential credit (25D) unavailable for property placed in service after Dec 31, 2025. Commercial credit deadlines for third-party systems: begin construction by July 4, 2026 or be in service by end of 2027.US tax credit guide
IndiaPM Surya Ghar central subsidy: ₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW, ₹18,000 for the third kW, up to ₹78,000. Higher rates in special-category states.Active. Apply through the national portal; domestic-content equipment required.PM Surya Ghar guide
United Kingdom0% VAT on installed residential solar and batteries; Smart Export Guarantee payments for exports.0% VAT until 31 March 2027, then 5%. SEG rates set by suppliers; MCS certification usually required.UK solar guide
AustraliaSTC discount on panels; Cheaper Home Batteries discount on batteries.STC value falls each January until the scheme ends Dec 31, 2030. Battery certificates per kWh step down every six months and taper above 14 kWh.Australia rebates guide
CanadaNo current federal homeowner solar grant or loan. Provincial, utility and municipal programs.Greener Homes Grant closed; Greener Homes Loan fully committed. New affordability program focuses on heat pumps and insulation.Canada incentives guide
