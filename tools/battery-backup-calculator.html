How battery sizing works

Storage needed = daily usage × share that is essential × (backup hours ÷ 24) ÷ usable depth of discharge. Lithium iron phosphate batteries typically allow 90 to 100% of their rated capacity to be used.

Capacity (kWh) decides how long you last; power (kW) decides what can run at once. A well pump, air conditioner or electric range can need 3 to 5 kW on start-up, so check the battery's peak rating against your largest motor load.

With solar, you need less

During a multi-day outage, panels recharge the battery each sunny day, so a battery sized for one night of essential loads often covers longer events. Without solar, size for the full outage.

Frequently asked

Is a home battery worth it financially?

Mainly where exported solar earns little, where peak-time rates are high, or where a rebate lowers the price. As pure backup it is insurance, valued differently by each household.

Can a battery run my whole house?

It can if it is sized for it, but whole-home backup often needs two or three modules. Most owners back up a critical-loads panel instead.
