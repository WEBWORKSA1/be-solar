Solar for renters and apartments: community solar and plug-in kits

By the Be.Solar editorial team. Updated October 21, 2026.

No roof of your own? Community solar subscriptions, balcony panels and landlord deals can still cut your bill.

Rooftop solar has mostly been for detached homeowners. If you rent, live in an apartment or have an unsuitable roof, there are still ways in.

Community solar

A community solar project is a shared solar farm, typically in your utility territory. You subscribe to a share of its output and receive credits on your electricity bill, usually at a discount of 5 to 15% off what that power would cost. No installation, and many plans let you cancel with notice. Availability depends on state or regional programs; in the US, a number of states have active programs.

What to check: the guaranteed discount, the contract length and cancellation fees, whether payments rise over time, and what happens if you move.

Plug-in and balcony solar

Small kits of one to four panels with a microinverter that plugs into a wall socket are popular in Germany, where millions are registered. They typically produce a few hundred kWh a year, enough to cover a home's constant background load. Rules differ by country: some limit output or require registration, others don't yet allow plug-in connection. Check local rules before buying.

Working with a landlord

Some landlords will install solar if the numbers are clear. Share a calculator estimate and a quote. Arrangements include the landlord owning the system and charging a modest rent increase, or a shared-savings agreement. For apartment buildings, a common-area system can cut service charges, and in India the PM Surya Ghar scheme offers support for housing societies' common facilities.

Other ways to cut your bill

  • Choose a green or time-of-use tariff and shift use to cheaper hours.
  • Switch to LED lighting and efficient appliances.
  • Use smart plugs to kill standby loads.
Planning to buy a home later? Run the savings calculator on listings you're considering. A south-facing, unshaded roof is a real long-term asset.

Frequently asked

Can I take community solar with me if I move?

Often yes within the same utility territory; otherwise most contracts allow cancellation, sometimes with notice or a fee.

Is plug-in solar safe?

Certified kits designed for plug-in use shut off when unplugged and are limited in output. Use only certified equipment and follow local rules.
