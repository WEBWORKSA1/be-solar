Solar panel maintenance and cleaning: what's actually needed

By the Be.Solar editorial team. Updated October 21, 2026.

How to keep a system producing for 25+ years with minimal effort, and when cleaning is worth paying for.

Solar panels have no moving parts and need little care. A few habits keep a system near its expected output for decades.

Watch the numbers

Your monitoring app is the best maintenance tool. Compare each month's production with the same month last year. A drop of more than 10% without obvious weather reasons deserves a look. Microinverter systems show each panel separately, making faults easy to locate.

Cleaning

Rain cleans most tilted panels well enough. Studies of soiling losses generally find a few percent in typical climates, but much more in dry, dusty regions, near farms, deserts or construction, and under bird droppings. Practical guidance:

  • Clean when you can see dirt or production has dipped, not on a fixed schedule.
  • Use soft water and a soft brush or sponge from the ground with an extension pole. Avoid pressure washers, abrasive cleaners and walking on panels.
  • Clean in early morning or evening; cold water on hot glass can stress it.
  • In dusty climates such as north India or inland Australia, monthly cleaning in the dry season can pay for itself.

Annual checks

  • Look for cracked glass, discoloured cells or loose wiring visible from the ground.
  • Trim trees that have grown into the sun path.
  • Check under the array for nesting birds or rodents; mesh guards are inexpensive.
  • Look at inverter lights or error codes.

When to call a professional

  • Inverter faults or errors that don't clear on restart.
  • Any burning smell, scorch marks or damaged cables.
  • After major storms or roof work.
  • A professional inspection every few years is sensible for older systems.

Expected lifespan

Panels typically degrade around 0.3 to 0.5% a year. String inverters often need replacing after 10 to 15 years; microinverters often carry 25-year warranties. Budget for one inverter replacement over the life of a string system.

Frequently asked

Does snow damage panels?

Panels are rated for snow loads, and snow usually slides off tilted panels within days. Don't scrape them with metal tools.

Can I use a garden hose?

Yes, with soft water from the ground. Hard water can leave spots; a final rinse with deionised water helps.
