Australian solar rebates in 2026: STCs, battery discounts and the 2030 wind-down

By the Be.Solar editorial team. Updated October 21, 2026.

How small-scale technology certificates cut your price, why they shrink each year, and how the battery discount works.

Australia has some of the world's cheapest home solar, helped by an upfront discount that the federal Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme (SRES) has delivered for years. That discount now shrinks each year until the scheme ends.

How STCs work

Installing an eligible system creates small-scale technology certificates (STCs) based on its expected output over a "deeming period" up to 2030. Installers usually claim the certificates and subtract their value from your price, so you see it as a discount rather than a cheque.

The wind-down to 2030

The SRES ends on 31 December 2030, so the deeming period, and with it the number of certificates, drops each year:

Cheaper Home Batteries program

Since 1 July 2025, eligible home batteries also earn STCs, which installers pass on as a discount of roughly 30% at launch. From May 2026 the certificate amount per kWh tapers for larger batteries: full rate for the first 14 kWh of usable capacity, reduced rates for capacity up to 50 kWh, and none beyond. The certificates per kWh also step down every six months until 2030, so the discount is largest for earlier installs.

State programs

Some states add their own rebates, interest-free loans or virtual power plant incentives for batteries. These change frequently; check your state energy department's website.

Feed-in tariffs

Payments for exported power have fallen to a few cents per kWh with many retailers, and some charge for exports in the middle of sunny days. That makes self-consumption and batteries increasingly attractive.

Buying tips

  • Use an installer accredited through Solar Accreditation Australia and an approved seller to receive STCs.
  • Choose panels and inverters from the Clean Energy Council approved products list.
  • Get three quotes; compare price per watt after STCs.

Frequently asked

Should I install before the STC value falls?

Each January the deeming period drops by a year, reducing the discount. If you were already planning to install, doing it earlier captures more value, but don't rush into a poor quote.

Can I get STCs on a battery added to existing solar?

Under the Cheaper Home Batteries program, eligible batteries installed by accredited installers can earn certificates whether or not you add panels at the same time.
