Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase or payment necessary to enter or win.
  • Open to individuals aged 18 or over, or 13–17 with written parental consent for the student challenge, where permitted by law. Void where prohibited.
  • Entries must be original, lawful and free of third-party trademarks as the main subject.
  • Judged by the Be.Solar editorial team on originality, usefulness and quality. Judges' decisions are final.

  • Prizes are non-transferable; cash equivalents may be substituted. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  • Personal data is used only to administer the contest; see our privacy policy.
