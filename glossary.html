Solar glossary

The terms you'll meet in quotes and contracts, in one line each.

AC / DC
Panels produce direct current (DC); homes and the grid use alternating current (AC). The inverter converts between them.
Azimuth
The compass direction a roof or panel faces. South (in the northern hemisphere) collects the most energy.
Battery depth of discharge (DoD)
The share of a battery's capacity that can be used. Most lithium iron phosphate batteries allow 90–100%.
Cost per watt
Installed price divided by system size in watts; the standard way to compare quotes.
DISCOM
An electricity distribution company in India. It approves rooftop solar connections and net meters.
Feed-in tariff
Payment per kWh for power exported to the grid, common in Australia.
Inverter
Equipment that converts DC from panels to AC for the home. Types include string, optimiser-based, micro and hybrid.
kW / kWp
Kilowatt: power. kWp (kilowatt-peak) is a system's rated output under standard test conditions.
kWh
Kilowatt-hour: energy. What your bill measures.
MCS
UK certification scheme for installers and products; usually needed for export payments.
Microinverter
A small inverter mounted on each panel, letting each work independently.
Net metering
Billing that credits exported power, often at the retail rate.
Net billing
Billing that credits exported power at a lower rate than retail.
Peak sun hours
Daily solar energy at a location expressed as hours of 1,000 W/m² sunshine.
Performance ratio
Real-world output as a share of theoretical output after losses, typically 75–85%.
Permission to operate (PTO)
Utility approval to switch on a grid-connected system.
PPA
Power purchase agreement: a company owns the panels on your roof and sells you the power.
SEG
Smart Export Guarantee: UK requirement for larger suppliers to pay for exported solar power.
Self-consumption
The share of solar output used on site rather than exported.
SREC
Solar renewable energy certificate, tradable in some US state markets.
STC
Small-scale technology certificate: Australia's upfront discount mechanism for solar and batteries.
Temperature coefficient
How much panel output drops for each degree Celsius above 25 °C.
Time-of-use (TOU) rate
A tariff where price per kWh varies by time of day.
Virtual power plant (VPP)
A network of home batteries coordinated to support the grid, often paying participants.
