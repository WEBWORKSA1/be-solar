Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Be.Solar ("we", "us") collects, how we use it and your choices. It applies to be.solar and its pages.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through forms: quote requests (name, email, phone, address, property and energy details), contact messages, newsletter signups, contest entries, job applications, pledges and sponsorship inquiries.
  • Information collected automatically by our hosting provider and by third-party services such as Google: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring site and cookie identifiers.
  • Calculator inputs stay in your browser and are not sent to us unless you submit a form.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and provide what you asked for.
  • For quote requests: to share your request with up to three solar installers serving your area, who may contact you about your project, as you consented on the form.
  • To send the newsletter if you subscribed (unsubscribe at any time).
  • To run contests, consider applications, and process pledges.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers or unrelated marketers.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our team through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service, which processes the data on our behalf to deliver it by email.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message managed by a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads are served. Our own banner lets you limit cookies to essential ones. We also store small preferences (theme, consent choice) in your browser's local storage.

YouTube videos load only when you press play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

Sharing

We share information with partner installers (quote requests only), service providers that help us run the site (hosting, form delivery, email), Google for advertising, and authorities when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose, generally up to 24 months, unless the law requires longer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California law, Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your information, and to withdraw consent. Use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". To stop installer contact, tell the installer and us.

Children

The site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; student contest entries from minors require parental consent.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above.
